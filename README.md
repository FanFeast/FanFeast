## :wave: Hi, I'm Mukul Ganwal

**Applied Scientist** at **[Maven Robotics](https://www.mavenrobotics.ai/)**, working on perception and robot learning for dextrous manipulation. Previously **Senior Perception Engineer & Team Lead** at **Anyware Robotics**, and MS at **Carnegie Mellon University**.

- :robot: Working on learning-based methods for robot manipulation: world models, policy learning, and 3D perception.
- :eyes: 2.5 years shipping real-time multi-camera RGB-D perception to production robots (segmentation, 6-DoF box pose, bulge-aware modeling).
- :zap: Deployment-minded: TensorRT/ONNX on Jetson and GPU, active-learning MLOps, synthetic data at scale.
- :page_facing_up: Co-inventor on a U.S. patent for multi-purpose warehouse robotic systems.
- :seedling: Interested in VLAs, embodied foundation models, and turning research into robots that actually ship.
